Community Terms

Timber is developed by Montgomery Software Foundry Inc. Version 2026-09-30.

These terms apply to Timber’s social features: searchable usernames, buddy requests, buddy lists, and Chops. You accept them in the app before your username becomes visible to other people. If you do not accept, you can keep logging workouts and nobody can find you.

What is not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, bullying, or targeting another person, including repeated unwanted requests or Chops.
  • Hateful, discriminatory, sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise offensive usernames.
  • Impersonating another person, business, or Timber.
  • Spam, scams, fake accounts, or promoting products and services.
  • Sharing another person’s private information.
  • Anything illegal, or anything that encourages others to hurt themselves.

Report and block

In the app, tap the “more” button beside any username on the Social tab to Report or Block that person, or to decline a request or remove a buddy.

  • Blocking ends any buddy connection. The two of you cannot find each other, send requests, or Chop each other. The person is not told. Unblock in About → App → Blocked Users.
  • Reporting sends the developer the reported account, your chosen reason, and any note you add. The reported person is not told who reported them.

What we do about reports

The developer reviews reports. We may dismiss a report, or remove the account from social features, or delete the account. We may do this without notice when the behavior is serious. Reports are kept for moderation and safety even if the reporter later deletes their account.

You can also email dev@adam-montgomery.ca. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Turning social features off

You can turn social features off in About → App. Your username stays reserved and people cannot find you, but existing buddy relationships are kept. Delete your account to remove them.

Changes

If these terms change materially, the app asks you to accept the new version before your username is visible again.
